Accessibility Statement

Last updated: {{lastUpdatedEn}}

We want Arnaki to be easy for everyone to use, including people with disabilities. The arnaki.app website was made accessible in line with Israel's Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998, the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013, and Israeli Standard SI 5568 "Web Content Accessibility", at level AA. The standard is based on the W3C's WCAG guidelines, and the site was also checked against their current version (WCAG 2.2) at level AA.

Accessibility features on this site

  • Pages have a clear heading structure and marked regions (header, main content, footer), plus a "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a clearly visible focus indicator and no keyboard traps.
  • Color contrast meets at least level AA, and no information is conveyed by color alone.
  • Each page declares its language and text direction (Hebrew right-to-left, English left-to-right) and has a unique title.
  • Text can be enlarged to 200% and beyond without losing content or functionality, and the layout adapts to small screens and zoomed views (responsive).
  • Decorative elements are hidden from screen readers, and all important information is available as text. There are no images that carry information.
  • Links have clear text, and FAQs use expandable sections that work with a keyboard and screen readers.
  • The site respects your device's "reduce motion" setting and contains no flashing content, videos or auto-playing content.
  • The site is always shown in light mode; a high-contrast mode is available from the accessibility menu.

The accessibility menu

Every page has a fixed accessibility button (the accessibility symbol, in the bottom corner of the screen). It works with a keyboard and screen readers and opens a menu with these adjustments:

  • Text size: three steps up to 150%, and back down.
  • High contrast: black text on a white background, with dark-blue underlined links.
  • Highlight links and highlight headings: underlines and outlines that make them stand out.
  • Readable font: replaces the designed fonts with a plain system font.
  • Text spacing: more space between lines, letters and words.
  • Stop motion: turns off all animations and transitions.

Your choices are saved on your device only (never sent to us), apply to every page of the site, and can be reset with one click. Browser and operating-system settings such as larger fonts or increased contrast are supported as well.

How we tested

The latest check was carried out on {{lastUpdatedEn}} and included an automated scan of every page with axe-core (WCAG 2.2 level AA checks) with no findings, keyboard-only navigation, and testing in an up-to-date version of Chrome on desktop and in mobile view, including 200% zoom. This statement is reviewed and updated at least once a year and whenever the site changes significantly.

Known limitations

The screen illustrations on the home page are for illustration only and are hidden from screen readers; everything they show is also written in the text beside them. This statement covers the arnaki.app website. The app's accessibility is checked separately; if you run into a problem in the app, please let us know and we'll fix it.
