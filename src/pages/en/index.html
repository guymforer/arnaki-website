How it works

  1. First, add your cards.

    Gift cards, holiday vouchers and store credit, plus the type of your credit and club cards, without their numbers.

  2. At the till, open Smart Pay.

    Pick the store, enter the amount, and get the cheapest way to pay.

  3. What you don't need, list in the market.

    Whoever wants the card writes to you in chat, and you agree between you. PayZo is not a party to the deal, and no payments or card transfers happen in the app.

Your cards stay yours

What happens to the numbers you keep in PayZo, in short.

PayZo · פייזו

When you give an app your card numbers, you deserve to know what happens to them. So here it is, no small print.

Card numbers and codes are stored encrypted and shown only to the card's owner. For benefits you choose only the type of credit card, never its number.

No ads and no trackers, and we never sell data. Every seller in the market is approved before they can list a card.

And if you want to leave: delete your account from inside the app, at any time. You can also report and block a user.
