3. What we don't collect

3.1 We don't collect credit card or bank account numbers, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and we don't use advertising identifiers. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics tools.

3.2 In the current version we don't ask for an ID number or a picture of an identity document.

4. Do you have to give us the information?

4.1 There's no legal obligation to give us information; giving it is up to you.

4.2 Without account details (name, email, phone and password) you can't open an account. You can browse the marketplace and coupons as a guest without giving any details. Without card details you can't save a card in the wallet or get recommendations for it. Without a full name you can't get seller approval. Without allowing notifications you won't be notified about new messages, and the rest of the service works as usual.

5. How we use the information

  • To run the service: manage the account, store and show the cards, calculate payment recommendations, show perks and coupons, run the marketplace and chat, and send service messages.
  • For safety and fraud prevention: approve sellers, handle reports, enforce the terms, limit misuse and secure the service.
  • To improve the service: fix faults using error logs, and improve the quality of payment recommendations based on the choices users make, using the data in aggregate form as far as possible.
  • To comply with the law: keep records the law requires, respond to a competent authority and defend against claims.

5.1 We don't sell personal information, don't share it with advertisers, and don't build profiles for advertising.

6. What other users see

6.1 Other users see the name you chose, the details of the listings you published (brand, amount and price, without the card number or code), and the messages in your conversations with them.

6.2 Your phone number isn't shown to other users unless you choose to share it in a particular conversation. You can stop sharing at any time, but someone who already saw the number may have kept it.

6.3 If you give a card's details to another user outside the service, that's your choice, and what they do with them is outside our control.

The transfer is made under the Privacy Protection (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations, 5761-2001: to countries whose law ensures an adequate level of protection, such as EU member states, or on the basis of the provider's contractual undertaking to protect the information and not pass it to others.

9. Information security

9.1 What we actually do:

  • all communication between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS);
  • card numbers, PINs and security codes are stored encrypted with AES-256 and are not written to logs;
  • passwords are stored only as a one-way hash (bcrypt);
  • a password reset code is valid for 15 minutes, single-use and limited in attempts;
  • changing your password signs out all devices, and signing out ends the session on the server;
  • rate limits on sensitive actions, and access to information limited to staff who need it;
  • in the app: the sign-in token is kept in the device's secure storage, the app screen is blurred in the app switcher, and a copied card number is cleared from the clipboard after 60 seconds.

9.2 No system is completely immune. If a security incident affects your information, we'll act under the law, including reporting to the Privacy Protection Authority and notifying you where the law requires.

9.3 You have a part too: choose a strong password you don't use elsewhere, and tell us right away if you suspect unauthorized access.

11. Deleting your account

11.1 You can delete your account at any time from inside the app (Profile → Delete account), or by asking at {{privacyEmail}}. Deletion is final and immediate.

11.2 Deleted immediately: your cards, store credits, perk cards, listings, phone number, seller approval and preferences. Your name and email are replaced with values that don't identify you, your password is cancelled, and your devices are signed out.

11.3 Kept, as set out in section 10: messages you sent (with the people who received them, under "Deleted user"), reports, records the law requires or that are needed to prevent fraud and defend against claims, and backups until they're deleted. The record that you accepted the terms is kept on the anonymized account.

11.4 Before deleting, save your card details outside the app, because after deletion we can't restore them.

12. Your rights

12.1 Under the Privacy Protection Law, you're entitled:

  • to view the information about you that we hold (section 13 of the Law);
  • to ask us to correct or delete information that is not accurate, complete, clear or up to date (section 14 of the Law);
  • to ask us to delete your account and information, as set out in section 11;
  • to ask to be removed from direct mailing, if you agree to receive it in the future (section 17F of the Law);
  • to complain to the Privacy Protection Authority at the Ministry of Justice.

12.2 To exercise a right, write to {{privacyEmail}} from the address registered on the account. We may ask for more details to confirm that you're the account holder. We'll reply within 30 days. If we refuse a request, we'll explain why.

12.3 If you ask to correct a detail that can't be changed in the app, such as your name, email or phone number, we'll correct it at your request.

13. Automated decisions

13.1 Smart Pay recommendations are calculated automatically, by estimated savings, how much of the amount is covered and the number of cards. They're recommendations only; whether to act on them is your decision.

13.2 Chat messages pass an automatic filter for offensive words. In addition, the number of certain actions is limited automatically, such as balance checks, sign-in attempts and listings.

13.3 Decisions on seller approval, suspension and handling reports are made by a person.

14. Minimum age

14.1 The service is for people aged 18 and over, and we don't knowingly collect information about minors. If we learn that a minor has opened an account, we'll delete it. A parent who believes their child opened an account can contact us.

15. Notifications and mailing

15.1 We send service messages only: a password reset code by email, and a phone notification about a new chat message. The notification shows the sender's name only, without the message content. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

15.2 We won't send you advertising without your explicit prior consent. If you agree in the future, you can withdraw consent at any time, through the link in the message or by contacting us.

16. The website and storage on your device

16.1 The arnaki.app website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising tools. The accessibility menu saves your choices in your own browser only. The website's hosting provider may keep basic technical records, such as IP addresses, for security.

16.2 The app stores on your device the sign-in token (in secure storage), language and country settings, and a favorites list. Card details are not stored on the device. When you sign out, the information stored on the device is cleared, except the language settings.
